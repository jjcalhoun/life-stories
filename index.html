A Life in Your Own Words

❦

Welcome. This is a place to set down your life story — at your own pace, in your own words.

To begin, or to pick up where you left off, tell us who you are. Your name and date of birth are how we’ll find your saved answers when you return.

New here? See what you’ll create.

Take a peek at an example life story book to see how your words and photos come together.

Are you filling this out on your own, or as a couple?

Coming back? Just enter your own name and date of birth below and press continue — even if you started as a couple, either of you can sign in alone and we’ll bring up your shared story right where you left off.

Either of you can sign in later with your own name and date of birth to reach the same shared story.

Your progress saves automatically as you go.
